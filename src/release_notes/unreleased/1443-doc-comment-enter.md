feature: Enter continues a doc comment, as in VS Code: after `/**` the new line starts with ` * ` and the block gets its closing ` */` if nothing closes it yet; a ` * …` line inside a block comment continues with ` * `; a ` */` line steps back to the column of its `/**`; and in Rust, `///` and `//!` lines continue with `/// ` and `//! `. This applies to JavaScript, TypeScript, C, C++, Java, C# and Rust; plain `//` comments are not continued.
