feature: Find in Selection, as in VS Code: `Alt+L` in the Find / Replace bar (or "Find: Toggle Find in Selection" in the palette) limits the matches, the count, Enter / Shift+Enter, the highlights and Replace / Replace All to the selected text, shown by a lit `≡` on the query row. Selecting one function and replacing `x` with `n` now rewrites that function only, and replacements of a different length keep the range right. VS Code's `toggleFindInSelection` keybinding imports onto it.
