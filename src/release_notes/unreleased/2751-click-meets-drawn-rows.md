fix: a click that arrives together with a wheel step, or with a key or click that moves or changes a list's rows, now lands on the row that was on screen, wherever that row moved, and on nothing once it is gone, instead of on a row that slid under the pointer. This covers the Explorer, Run and Debug, Problems, Open Editors, Outline, Search and the Remote Explorer, where a filter key and a double-click could otherwise connect to a host that was never shown, and matters most over SSH, where input arrives in bursts (#2751).
