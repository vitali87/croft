feature: Files without a byte-order mark can now open in a legacy encoding such as windows-1252, latin1 or Shift_JIS instead of always as UTF-8. croft follows the `.editorconfig` `charset` and a new `files_encoding` setting (VS Code's `files.encoding`, also read from `.vscode/settings.json`), and a status-bar encoding choice is remembered for that file, so it survives closing and reopening the tab (#1432).
