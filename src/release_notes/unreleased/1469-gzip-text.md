fix: A single gzipped file, such as a rotated `app.log.1.gz`, `data.json.gz` or `dump.sql.gz`, now opens as its text in a read-only view, under its own name, instead of as a hex dump of the compressed bytes. A save never writes the text over the archive. A gzip of binary data, or one that would decompress to more than 256 MiB, still opens as hex, and a `.tar.gz` still opens in the archive browser.
