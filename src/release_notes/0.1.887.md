feature: Type `ssh box` in a pane and, when `box` is in your ~/.ssh/config, the status bar offers to open the workspace on that host: Cmd+K G accepts, Esc dismisses, and the offer goes away when the session ends. Turn it off with `disable_remote_offer`, per host with `remote_offer_excluded_hosts`; a host croft could not provision is left alone for a week (#364).
