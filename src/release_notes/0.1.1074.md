feature: a config file you edit on your own machine during a `croft remote` session (keybindings, snippets, triggers, problem matchers, macros) is pushed to the remote within a couple of seconds and applied there, instead of waiting for the next connect. It follows `config_sync_excluded_hosts` and `config_sync_excluded_files`, and logs to `~/.cache/croft/config-sync.log` (part of #262).
