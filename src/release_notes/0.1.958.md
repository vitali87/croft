feature: the number under the cursor can be incremented and decremented with `Cmd+Opt+=` and `Cmd+Opt+-` — vim's `Ctrl-A` and `Ctrl-X`. The caret does not have to be on the number; the next one along the line is used. Zero padding keeps its width (`007` becomes `008`), and hex and binary literals keep their prefix and digit case (`0xFF` becomes `0x100`). Also in the command palette as Increment / Decrement Number Under Cursor.
