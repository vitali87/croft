feature: Terminal panes can outlive croft. With "terminal_persistent_panes": true (Linux), each shell pane runs in a process of its own, so when croft is killed or crashes, for example by the out-of-memory killer, your shells and the jobs in them keep running. The next croft in the workspace reattaches to them with the screen and recent output redrawn. Closing a pane or quitting croft still ends its shell (#694).
