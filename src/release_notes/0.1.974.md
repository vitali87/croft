feature: Go debugging, through delve. F5 on a `.go` file debugs its package, and on a `_test.go` file runs the package's tests under the debugger. A `launch.json` configuration with `"type": "go"` launches or attaches, honouring delve's `mode` (`debug`, `test`, `exec`, or `local` to attach to a `processId`). Breakpoints, conditions, logpoints, stepping, the call stack, variables and watches work as they do for the other languages. croft finds `dlv` on your PATH, in `$GOBIN` or in `$GOPATH/bin`. Without it, croft tells you the `go install` command to run.
