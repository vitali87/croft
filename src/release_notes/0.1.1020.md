feature: `.sarif` files open in a results viewer. Results are grouped by file, rule or log, with level, baseline and suppression filters and a search box (`rule:`, `file:`, `level:`, `tag:`, `-term`, `a|b`). Enter opens a result at its line and column and keeps the viewer tab open. A log that is not SARIF 2.1.0, or not valid JSON, opens as text with the reason.
